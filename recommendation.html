Integrated Recommendation

Section owner: Group, led by RATUL HASAN MUSA

This section gives an overall judgement on whether the project is on track to deliver before the trading peak.

Overall judgement on readiness

The project is viable and close to plan. The schedule finishes the customer-facing rollout on 19 November 2026, ahead of the trading freeze, and the cost and schedule variances at the status date are small and recoverable. The main threat to readiness is the ageing ERP integration, which sits on the path to testing and go-live.

Major unresolved issues

The open concerns are the ERP integration defect backlog, the UAT slippage, and the tight buffer before the freeze. None is severe on its own, but together they leave little room for a further delay on the critical path.

Professional recommendation

Proceed, with three conditions: hold the extra integration and QA capacity until UAT is stable, keep a firm change freeze before the trading peak, and review earned value weekly. On the evidence, the project can meet its go-live target if the critical path is protected through the rollout.
