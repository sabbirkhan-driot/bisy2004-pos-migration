Quality, Change and Delivery

Section owner: Sabbir khan

This section describes the quality targets, the method of change control, and the Kanban workflow used to deliver the work.

Quality requirements and metrics

MetricTargetMeasurement
POS transaction success rateAt least 99.5 percentTransaction logs during hypercare
Average checkout transaction time6 seconds or lessSampled timing tests per store
Open critical or major defects at go-liveZeroDefect register at gate 3
UAT pass rate before test gateAt least 95 percentUAT results at gate 2
Store uptime during hypercareAt least 99.5 percentMonitoring dashboard
Price and inventory data accuracy100 percent reconciledMigration reconciliation report

Change control

Changes are handled through short change requests. One request, for example, added integration effort to clear an ERP defect backlog, and another brought security testing forward to run in parallel with UAT. Each was assessed against the baseline before approval.

Kanban workflow

The delivery workflow was managed on a Trello board. Cards were titled with their WBS code and moved across lists from backlog to done, with labels for phase and owner and checklists for the steps in each task, which kept the team's daily work tied to the WBS and the schedule.
