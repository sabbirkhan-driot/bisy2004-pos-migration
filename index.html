Project Overview

This site presents the plan and controls for a national retail chain's cloud POS migration, with the evidence for each area drawn from the project tools.

18 September 2026

Business problem and purpose

The in-store point of sale is on old on-premise hardware. These systems offer limited real-time inventory, reporting and omnichannel capability, are expensive to maintain, and are not always reliable. The business requires a working, stable cloud POS in time for the trading rush of December to January.

The project provides and deploys a cloud based point of sale solution in a first region, integrated with payments and the ERP inventory system, enabling the stores to trade reliably during the peak and the business to have live stock and reporting.

Methodology and life cycle

The project is based on a predictive life cycle and has four phase gates. It is suitable for work that has a fixed target date and clear deliverables. It allows a schedule, critical path, baseline and earned value to be planned and tracked with accuracy. Gate reviews at planning, testing and go-live give clear control points.

Key objectives

  • Install a cloud POS platform integrated with payments and the ERP stock system, passing test gate 2 by 28 September 2026.
  • Complete the first-region rollout in two waves with all in-scope stores live before the trading freeze, by 19 November 2026.
  • Maintain a high transaction success rate of 99.5 percent or higher and checkout time within target during hypercare.
  • Deliver within the approved baseline of AUD 557,172, plus or minus 10 percent.
