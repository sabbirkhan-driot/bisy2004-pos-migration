Schedule, Critical Path and Cost

Section owner: RATUL HASAN MUSA

This section shows the schedule, the critical path and the cost baseline, and explains what they mean for go-live.

Gantt chart and baseline

Critical path

Because the critical path has no spare time, any delay to a critical task delays go-live. The most important scheduling control is protecting the critical path, since the customer-facing rollout finishes on 19 November, only days before the trading freeze.

Cost and interpretation

The baseline cost is AUD 557,172, made up of AUD 265,520 labour, AUD 241,000 non-labour, and a 10 percent contingency of AUD 50,652. Labour is the largest controllable expense, and most of it sits in the build and test phases, so overruns there have the greatest effect on the budget.
